What it does

Clip Generator is a private tool run by the owner of the YouTube channels listed above. It cuts short vertical highlight clips from public videos, always credits the original creator on screen and in the caption, and uploads the clips the owner picks to the owner's own YouTube channel as Shorts.

How it uses your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Clip Generator asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the clips you choose to your own channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel you signed in with before anything is uploaded.

Nothing is uploaded until you pick the clips and start the upload yourself. The sign-in token stays on your own computer. Read the privacy policy for details.
