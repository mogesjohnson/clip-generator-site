Clip Generator privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Clip Generator (home) uses YouTube API Services. By signing in you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Your YouTube channel's name and ID, read with youtube.readonly, to confirm which channel you signed in with.
  • Permission to upload videos with youtube.upload, used only for clips you select. It does not read, change or delete your existing videos.

How it stores and shares data

  • The Google sign-in token is saved only on the computer that runs Clip Generator. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Clip Generator keeps a local list of the video IDs it uploaded, so it does not upload the same clip twice.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. There are no analytics or cookies on this website.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access and data

  • Revoke access at any time on your Google Account permissions page.
  • Deleting the token file on the computer that runs Clip Generator removes all stored Google data. Uploaded videos stay on your channel until you delete them in YouTube Studio.
